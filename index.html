Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products

Wetsuit thickness depends on water temperature. For warm water (70°F+), a 2mm or 3/2mm suit is ideal. Temperate waters (60-70°F) need 3/2mm or 4/3mm. Cold water (50-60°F) requires 4/3mm or 5/4/3mm. For freezing conditions (below 50°F), consider a 6/5/4mm with hood, boots, and gloves.

A proper wetsuit should fit snugly like a second skin without restricting movement. There should be no gaps at the neck, wrists, or ankles where water can flush through. The suit will loosen slightly in water. If you can easily pull the neoprene away from your skin when dry, it's too big.

Always rinse your wetsuit with fresh water after each use. Hang dry inside out in shade (sunlight degrades neoprene). Store on a wide hanger or folded loosely. Avoid sharp objects that can tear the material. Use wetsuit shampoo occasionally to remove odors and bacteria. Never dry clean or machine wash.

We offer a 30-day return policy on unworn, unwashed merchandise with original tags attached. Wetsuits that have been in water cannot be returned for hygiene reasons. Please contact our customer service for return authorization before sending anything back.
